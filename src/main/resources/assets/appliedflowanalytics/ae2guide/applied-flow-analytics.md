# Applied Flow Analytics

Applied Flow Analytics tracks resource movement through your Applied Energistics 2 network.
Use it to see what enters and leaves the network, display flow graphs, and control redstone based on flow rates.

## Flow Terminal

<ItemImage id="appliedflowanalytics:flow_terminal" />

The Flow Terminal shows resource inflow, outflow, and net flow with detailed statistics and graphs.
Select a resource to explore how its flow changes over time.

## Flow Monitor

<ItemImage id="appliedflowanalytics:flow_monitor" />

The Flow Monitor displays a resource's flow graph directly in the world, so you can watch it without opening a screen.

## Flow Level Emitter

<ItemImage id="appliedflowanalytics:flow_level_emitter" />

The Flow Level Emitter emits a redstone signal based on configurable flow rates and thresholds.
Use it to make your automation respond to changes in resource flow.
